# scout-brief: user-discovery methodology (issue-1)

## Category must-bes (what any credible discovery practice requires)

- Falsifiable hypotheses stated before interviews start, with an explicit
  disconfirming observation named (Blank/Ries customer-development
  discipline).
- Questions about specific past behavior, not future hypotheticals or
  opinions about the idea (Fitzpatrick, *The Mom Test*).
- A defined stopping rule tied to thematic saturation, not a fixed
  headcount picked in advance (Guest, Bunce & Johnson 2006).
- Evidence graded by strength (observed/behavioral > recounted past event
  > opinion/prediction/compliment) before it's allowed to support a
  verdict (synthesized discovery-methodology consensus; Braun & Clarke
  2006 check-back step is the mechanism that catches confirmation bias).

## Chosen performance axes (2-3 dimensions strong practice competes on)

1. **Falsifiability of interview questions** — can a question's answer
   actually disconfirm the hypothesis, or is it structurally safe for
   the interviewee to be polite (Mom Test's core test).
2. **Evidence traceability** — can every claim in the final verdict be
   traced back to a specific interview segment and its evidence-strength
   tag, or does the verdict rest on vibes/aggregate impression.
3. **Saturation discipline** — is the sample size an output of a stated
   stopping rule, or a number picked before any interview happened.

## Adopt / skip

- **Adopt**: Mom Test question discipline + an explicit evidence-strength
  tag on every logged interview segment (behavioral > recounted >
  opinion). This is the highest-leverage, cheapest-to-encode norm and
  directly matches the role's mandate ("이 문제가 실제 사용자의 고통인가"
  cannot be answered from opinions).
- **Skip (deliberately)**: JTBD's full four-forces switch-interview
  model (push/pull/anxiety/habit) as a MANDATORY structure. It's a rich
  technique but adds interview complexity beyond what a pain-confirmed/
  not-confirmed verdict needs; the timeline/past-event backbone is worth
  adopting, the full forces diagram is not required scaffolding for this
  role's narrower mandate. Kept as an optional deepening technique, not
  a required section.

## Gap line: what current directive/plugin already meets vs. missing

- **Already meets**: names the three deliverables (interview script,
  per-interview evidence log, pain verdict) in `plugin.json`/`directive.sh`
  prose (see survey.md).
- **Missing**: no structural definition of any of the three deliverables,
  no evidence-strength rubric, no stopping rule, no REQUIRED_FIELDS-style
  enforcement (issue-2 removed the local gate; nothing yet replaces its
  role-specific config). See survey.md for the full inventory.
